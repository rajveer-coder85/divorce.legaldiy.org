You have both decided to divorce.

Now comes a different question:

What happens after the marriage ends?

A good agreement should reflect decisions that both spouses understand and can realistically follow.

## What Property Do You Have?

Start by identifying what actually exists.

Think about:

* matrimonial home;
* other property;
* savings and investments;
* vehicles;
* significant business interests;
* relevant retirement assets;
* loans and debts.

Then ask:

What do we agree should happen to them?

[Work Through Property &rarr;](/joint-petition/what-we-need-to-agree#property)
## Can the Agreement Actually Be Carried Out?

An agreement may sound simple:

"I will keep the house."

But practical questions remain:

* Is there a mortgage?
* Can the financing be changed?
* Does ownership need to be transferred?
* Are there costs involved?
* When should the transfer or sale happen?

Think beyond the sentence.

Understand how the arrangement would work in practice.

## Do Both of You Understand the Same Agreement?

Before moving forward, each spouse should be able to explain:

* what happens to the children;
* what maintenance will be paid, if any;
* what happens to the home;
* what happens to major assets and debts;
* what continuing responsibilities remain.

If your understanding is different, you may not actually have reached agreement yet.
