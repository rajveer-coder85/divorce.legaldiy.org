Court is often the part people worry about most.

The purpose of this page is to reduce uncertainty through education—not to provide a memorised court script.

## Why Is the Court Involved?

A civil marriage is dissolved through a legal court process. The High Court considers the Joint Petition, whether the legal requirements are satisfied and the arrangements the spouses ask it to recognise or order.

Agreement between spouses does not itself dissolve the marriage or guarantee that every proposed term will be accepted.

## What Should We Understand Before Court?

Both spouses should understand:

- the Joint Petition;
- the arrangements being proposed;
- children arrangements;
- maintenance arrangements;
- property arrangements; and
- what orders they are asking the court to make.

## Do We Need a Script?

The important preparation is understanding your own petition and proposed arrangements. Memorising answers that you do not understand is not a substitute for knowing what you are asking the court to do.

Attendance requirements and the questions raised can depend on current procedure, the court and the circumstances.

## What Happens If the Court Is Satisfied?

Current Malaysian Government guidance describes the court issuing a Provisional Court Order, known as the Decree Nisi. This is followed by a later finalisation stage; it is not yet the final divorce documentation.
