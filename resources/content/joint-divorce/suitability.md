Start with the most important question:

> Are both of you willing to approach the divorce together?

A Joint Petition is based on both spouses proceeding jointly. Before thinking about documents or court procedure, determine whether you actually have the foundation for an agreed divorce.

## Ask Yourselves

- We both want the marriage to end.
- We are both willing to proceed with the divorce together.
- We are willing to discuss the arrangements arising from the divorce.
- We are both able to make decisions freely.
- We understand that agreeing to divorce does not necessarily mean that we have already agreed on every term.
