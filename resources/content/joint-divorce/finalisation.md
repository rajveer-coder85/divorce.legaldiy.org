Attending court does not necessarily mean every final legal and administrative step has been completed.

## Provisional Court Order: Decree Nisi

Current Malaysian Government guidance says that, if the High Court is satisfied with a non-Muslim divorce application, it issues a **Provisional Court Order**, commonly called the **Decree Nisi**.

The Decree Nisi is provisional. It should not be treated as the final divorce certificate.

## Final Divorce Order: Decree Nisi Absolute

The same Government guidance states that, after three months, the spouses may apply for the **Decree Nisi Absolute**, also described as the **Certificate of Divorce**.

The timing or steps in an individual case can be affected by the court's order and current procedure. Check the documents and directions applicable to your matter rather than assuming that finalisation happens automatically.

## Updating Divorce Information With JPN

JPN currently states that updating the marriage record requires the original Decree Nisi Absolute, a court-certified true copy or the pertinent court e-filing document, together with the other required documents.

Keep copies of important divorce documentation. Current requirements should be checked directly before submission.

## After Finalisation

- Retain important court documents.
- Implement agreed property arrangements.
- Follow maintenance obligations.
- Follow children arrangements.
- Update relevant official records where required.
