Use this public educational checklist to identify what you have resolved and what still needs thought. It does not collect personal answers and does not determine legal eligibility or whether a court will approve an arrangement.

## 1. Divorce

- We both want the divorce.
- We both intend to proceed together.

## 2. Children, Where Applicable

- We have discussed the children's arrangements.
- We understand where they will live.
- We understand access.
- We have considered their financial needs.

## 3. Maintenance

- We have discussed child maintenance where applicable.
- We understand whether spousal maintenance is relevant.

## 4. Property

- We have identified our significant property and debts.
- We have discussed what should happen to them.

## 5. Understanding

- Both of us understand the arrangements.
- Neither of us is relying on hidden important financial information.
- Both of us are making the agreement voluntarily.
