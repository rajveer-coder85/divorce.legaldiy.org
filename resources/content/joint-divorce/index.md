If you and your spouse have both decided that the marriage should end, you may be able to approach the divorce together through a Joint Petition.

But agreeing to divorce is only the beginning.

Before proceeding, both of you should understand the decisions that need to be made about your children, financial support, property and the arrangements that will continue after the marriage ends.

LegalDIY helps you understand those decisions before you move forward.

## Do Both of You Want to Divorce?

A Joint Petition begins with both spouses being willing to proceed together.

You do not need to agree about why the marriage ended. But both of you need to be willing to approach the divorce together and work through the arrangements that need to be made.

## What If We Cannot Agree?

LegalDIY's current divorce education pathway focuses on spouses who are able to proceed together through a Joint Petition.

If one spouse does not agree to proceed jointly, or there is a serious unresolved dispute about children, maintenance or property, you may need a different approach.

This does not mean that you cannot get divorced. It means that the Joint Petition pathway may not currently fit your circumstances.
