You have both decided that you want to divorce.

Now ask:

> What happens after the marriage ends?

A Joint Petition should reflect arrangements that both spouses understand and genuinely agree to.

## Do You Have Children?

If yes, work through where the children will live, time with each parent, important decisions and their financial needs.

[Work Through Children Arrangements →](/joint-divorce/children)

## What About Financial Support?

Consider child maintenance and whether maintenance between spouses is relevant to your circumstances.

[Work Through Maintenance →](/joint-divorce/maintenance)

## Do You Own Property or Have Significant Assets?

Identify the home, other property, savings, investments, vehicles, relevant business or retirement interests, loans and debts.

[Work Through Property →](/joint-divorce/property)

## After Working Through These Areas

Check whether both spouses understand the same arrangements and whether the agreement is practical and voluntary.

[Check Your Agreement →](/joint-divorce/agreement)
