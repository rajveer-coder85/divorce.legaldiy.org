Once both spouses understand and agree on the arrangements, the next question is how the Joint Petition moves through the legal process.

This is a high-level educational overview. Exact requirements depend on current law, court procedure and the individual matter.

## The General Journey

1. **Understand and agree.** Work through children, maintenance, property and continuing responsibilities.
2. **Prepare the required divorce documents.** The documents must accurately reflect the spouses' information and proposed arrangements.
3. **File the Joint Petition.** The relevant documents are formally placed into the High Court process.
4. **Court process.** Both spouses should understand the petition and the orders they are asking the court to make.
5. **Court's decision and provisional divorce order.** If satisfied, the court may issue a Provisional Court Order, commonly called the Decree Nisi.
6. **Finalisation.** A later stage is required before the provisional order becomes the final divorce order.
7. **Update relevant civil records.** The final divorce documentation is used for the required JPN update.

## What This Public Guide Does Not Provide

This page does not provide individual document drafting, form numbers, filing-counter instructions, hearing scripts, guaranteed dates, fees or outcomes. Those matters can change or depend on the case.
